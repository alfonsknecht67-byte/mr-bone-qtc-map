MR. BONE — QTC On-chain Intelligence & Visualization

This project provides public on-chain analysis and visualization based on publicly available Qubitcoin blockchain data.

The Bubble Map is intended to make blockchain activity easier to explore by visualizing selected addresses, balances and reconstructed transaction relationships.

Transaction flows may include heuristic reconstruction and do not by themselves establish ownership or control of an address.
